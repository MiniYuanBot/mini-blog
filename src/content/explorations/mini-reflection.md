I don't know when I started confusing learning with passively **absorbing** an endless stream of information. Somewhere along the way, I seem to have lost the ability to **generate** my own tokens.

Maybe the best way to solve that is to make better use of my blog?

From now on, I want to spend more time thinking, writing, and actually working on something everyday.

I hope you will follow these five Ps (I've decided to call this my **VP plan**, hhh)：

- **Produce**: Become the producer of information rather than a consumer.
- **Practice**: Get your hands dirty rather than just reading blog posts.
- **Persist**: Believe in yourself and keep moving forward.
- **Physical & Psychological Healthy**: All work and no play makes miniyuan a dull boy.
- ~~Psycho~~

Of course, this doesn't mean I should stop using AI agents. I think I should make better use of them instead to handle some trivial course work, liberating myself to focus on things that actually deserve my attention.

Agent should augment my thinking, not replace it.

Make love, not prompts.
